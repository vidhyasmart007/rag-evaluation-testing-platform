Company Knowledge Base

The company office is located in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

The company uses Python as its primary programming language
for several internal automation and AI projects.

Employees receive 18 annual leave days per calendar year.

The AI team works on machine learning, large language models,
AI evaluation, and automation solutions.

The company encourages employees to improve their technical
skills through practical projects and continuous learning.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines information retrieval
with language generation. It retrieves relevant document
sections and uses them as context for answering questions.

Faithfulness measures whether an AI-generated answer is
supported by the provided context.

Context relevance measures whether the retrieved information
is relevant to the user's question.

The company employs approximately 120 people across engineering,
sales, and customer support.

The standard work week runs Monday through Friday.

The engineering team holds a planning meeting every Monday morning.

Code changes are reviewed through pull requests on GitHub before
they are merged into the main branch.

The evaluation suite is executed with pytest and runs as part of
the continuous integration pipeline.

Customer data is stored in an encrypted database that is backed up
every night.
